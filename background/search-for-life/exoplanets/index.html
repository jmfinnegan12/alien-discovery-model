K2-18b

K2-18b is a planet outside our solar system. It orbits a small, cool star 120 light-years from Earth, in the constellation Leo, and is 8.6 times as massive as Earth.1 At that distance it can only be studied by telescope.

In 2025, JWST picked up a hint of dimethyl sulfide in the planet's air, a gas made by life on Earth. The signal is weak, and non-living explanations are still open.2
