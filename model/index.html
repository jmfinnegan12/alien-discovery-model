Low and high cases mean a lower or higher chance of Yes, so for the steepest point the low case is the later year. P(Yes before date) = 1 − (1 − Science)(1 − Disclosure)(1 − Remark). Science = Exists and findable × Announced × S-curve share by that date. Disclosure and Remark are spread evenly from 2026-11-05 to the end of the term (2029-01-20).
